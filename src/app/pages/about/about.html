The Idea

Always in the 'idea' phase.

01 / DEVELOPMENT

"You only know what you know."

I started working on personal projects at home to fill in the knowledge gaps I found in my professional work. The best way to learn was to build somethings for myself.

02 / MAINTAIN

"Handle your own."

The greatest benefit of building my own applications was learning how to maintain them. Having to fix problems myself taught me how to troubleshoot problems I might not have had an opportunity to encounter at work.

03 / INFRASTRUCTURE

"Brick By Brick"

Designing infrastructure using mostly used hardware is challenging and rewarding. The idea is to understand every piece of the system intimately. It makes troubleshooting simpler and building more personal.

04 / THE JD SUITE

"All Together Now"

The JD Suite is essentially the culmination of all my ideas under the same brand. Putting my name on it inspires pride in my work and keeps the standards high.

"THE SUITE"

Understanding comes
from doing.

I learn best when there’s something real to build. All my software projects become an opportunity to work through design philosophies, and delivery.

I enjoy connecting those pieces, working through what breaks, and improving the result. The goal of The Suite is to build useful software and understand it well enough to keep it working while demystifying the tools that larger teams and companies use to tackle large scale issues.
